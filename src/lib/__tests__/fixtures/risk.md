2.7.1 Identify Risks

Risk identification is continuous. Anything that may, might or could happen and would matter if it did is a candidate for the risk register. Once an uncertain event has actually happened it stops being a risk and becomes an issue, and it moves to the issue log where corrective action is tracked.

2.7.2 Perform Risk Analysis

Analysis combines the qualitative ranking of probability and impact with quantitative modelling where the data supports it. The output is a prioritised list and, for the top risks, a quantified exposure that informs how much contingency reserve to hold.

2.7.3 Plan Risk Responses

Responses for threats include avoid, mitigate, transfer, escalate and accept. Escalation is used only when the response lies outside the project's authority; it is never the default first move. Each planned response names an owner and a trigger, and its cost is drawn from the contingency reserve when the risk is realised.
